    Frequently Asked Questions

    Have questions? We have answers.

    How do I get started? ▼
    Sign up for free, connect your social accounts, and start posting. It takes just 5 minutes to get your first post live.
    Which platforms are supported? ▼
    We support 10 platforms: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Medium, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, YouTube, and Reddit. More platforms coming soon.
    Can I edit posts after scheduling? ▼
    Yes! You can edit any scheduled post until it's published. Simply navigate to the post and make your changes.
    How does the AI optimization work? ▼
    Our AI analyzes platform specifications and automatically adjusts your content. Different captions, hashtags, and formats are created for each platform to maximize engagement.
    Can I cancel anytime? ▼
    Yes! You can cancel your subscription anytime with no questions asked. Your data is always yours and exportable.
    Is there API access? ▼
    API access is available with our Enterprise plan. Contact our sales team to learn more about custom integrations.
    What about team accounts? ▼
    Team accounts are available on our Professional and Enterprise plans. Invite team members and set different permission levels.
    Do you offer white-label options? ▼
    Yes! Our Enterprise plan includes white-label options. You can rebrand MultiPost AI as your own product and offer it to your clients.
